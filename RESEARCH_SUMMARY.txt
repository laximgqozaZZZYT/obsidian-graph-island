2. COORDINATE PIPELINE (src/layouts/coordinate-engine.ts)
   =====================================================
   Three phases:
   
   PHASE 1: resolveAxisValues() — lines 400-456
     Extracts raw numeric values based on AxisSource.kind:
     - index: sequential 0,1,2,...
     - field/property: frontmatter value or lexicographic index
     - metric: degree, in-degree, out-degree, bfs-depth, sibling-rank
     - hop: BFS distance from node
     - random: seeded hash
     - const: fixed value
     Output: Map<nodeId, number>
   
   PHASE 2: applyTransform() — lines 652-723
     Maps raw values through coordinate space:
     - linear: raw * scale
     - bin: quantize to equal-width bins
     - date-to-index: chronological sort
     - even-divide: distribute across range (degrees)
     - stack-avoid: vertical spreading within columns
     - golden-angle: golden spiral multiplier
     - expression: custom math expression
     - curve: parametric curve (archimedean, fermat, spiral, etc.)
     - shape-fill: pack into geometric shape
     Output: Map<nodeId, number>
   
   PHASE 3: toCartesian() — lines 733-772
     Converts axes to (dx, dy):
     - Cartesian: x=axis1, y=axis2
     - Polar: (x,y) = (r*cos(θ), r*sin(θ))
     - Centroid normalization: shift all by centroid
     Output: Map<nodeId, {dx, dy}>

3. AXIS SOURCES (src/types.ts, lines 84-91)

6. PRESETS (src/layouts/coordinate-presets.ts, lines 106-157)
   ===========================================================
   - concentric: polar, rings = floor(i/_ringSize)+1
   - radial: polar, spokes = (i%_spokeCount)*(360/_spokeCount)
   - phyllotaxis: polar, r=sqrt(i), θ=i*pi*(3-sqrt(5))
   - grid: cartesian, column=i%ceil(sqrt(n)), row=floor(i/ceil(sqrt(n)))
   - triangle: cartesian, triangular packing
   - random: cartesian, seeded random positions
   - timeline: cartesian, date on x, stack-avoid on y
   - custom: cartesian, user-defined field/metric axes

7. CURVE PRESETS (src/layouts/coordinate-presets.ts, lines 14-99)
   ==============================================================
   Parametric curves for spiral layouts:
   - archimedean: a + b*t (linear spiral)
   - logarithmic: a*exp(b*t*tau) (exponential spiral)
   - fermat: a*sqrt(t) (square root spiral)
   - hyperbolic: a/t (hyperbolic spiral)
   - cardioid: a*(1+cos(t*tau)) (heart shape)
   - rose: a*cos(k*t*tau) (flower petals)
   - lissajous: sin(a*t*tau+δ) (harmonic oscillation)
   - golden: a*1.618^(t*4) (golden ratio spiral)
